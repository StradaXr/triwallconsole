Start Tri-Wall Studio first

This page is the inside of the app, so it can't work on its own. Double-click start_triwall_studio.bat in the triwall-studio folder. Your browser will open the studio by itself.

  1. Open the folder D:\ComfyUI\triwall-studio
  2. Double-click start_triwall_studio.bat
  3. Keep the black window open while you work
